## Decision

Calcium nitrate and phosphate-sulfate stock solutions are dosed on separate lines with no shared plumbing or mixing point upstream of the reservoir.

## Why

Calcium nitrate combined with phosphate or sulfate salts at stock-solution concentration precipitates out as insoluble calcium phosphate or calcium sulfate, which clogs lines and removes nutrients from solution before the plants can use them. This is why commercial hydroponic nutrients ship as separate A/B (or A/B/C) parts rather than one combined concentrate.
