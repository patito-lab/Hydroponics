# 008: Plant spacing

**Date:** 8Aug26
**Status:** Accepted / Superseded by NNN

## Context

Need to determine how far apart plants sit and where to drill holes for net pots.

## Options considered

Considered using maximum length vs leaving extra space at the ends.

## Decision

Hole spacing from end:
Basil: 4.5"
Cilantro: 14"
Parsley: 22.5"
Chives: 29.5"

All are centered on the width

Holes are approximately 2 7/8" diameter

## Why

Overall length is 35", but the ends are curved with a ~4" radius, leaving a 27" straight section. Selected hole spacing only along the straight section, starting 1" in. This leaves the rounded sections empty for sensors, wire runs, and dosing.

The tighter spacing still leaves adequate space between plants (about 6", a litte more for basil as it's roots are bigger)
