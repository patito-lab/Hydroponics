# 006: liquid level

**Date:** 8Aug26
**Status:** Accepted

## Context

Need to determine where to mount float switches to measure liquid level

## Options considered

Ultrasonic sensor vs float switches for liquid mesasurement considered

## Decision

Float switches chosen. Mounted at 2", 3.5" and 5.5" Air stone will be included for aeration.

## Why

Ultrasonic sensor is expensice and more difficult to work with for a v1 build so it was rejected.

Float switches provide a min, max, and refill indication. 2" of liquid is enough to still cover the sensors, but will leave roots almost entirely exposed. 3.5" indicates a refill is needed, but roots are still mostly submerged. 5.5" reaches to the bottom of the net pot, wicking moisture for when plants are small. Any higher risks root rot.

Airstone helps aerate water and mix nutrients, removes need for an air gap to the crown of the plants.
